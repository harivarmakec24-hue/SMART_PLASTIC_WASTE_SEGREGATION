Why Smart Waste Segregation?

Automated computer vision segregation reduces landfill dumping and boosts municipal recycling efficiency.

Environmental Protection

Prevents toxic plastic contamination in oceans and preserves wildlife natural habitats.

Energy Conservation

Recycling aluminum cans saves up to 95% of energy required to mine raw bauxite ore.
